About us

A small studio with big worlds in it.

Anemos Interactive is an independent game development and technology studio. We make our own games, we build games and software for partners, and we do all of it online, from Mumbai, for players and clients anywhere.

How we work

Built like a game studio, run like a business

Fully online

Our games reach players through online storefronts, and our client work is delivered remotely, from the first meeting to the final build. No office visits needed, anywhere in the world.

Engine-deep

We work from the top of the stack to the bottom: full games in Unity and Unreal, 3D assets in Blender, and C++ and C# down to the rendering layer, on OpenGL and DirectX.

Story first

Our own titles are story-driven. The first, It Still Beats, is a thriller built for PC and console storefronts, and the same care for craft goes into everything we make for others.

AI-assisted tools, human-made work

We use AI tools where they speed us up: research, first drafts, iteration. Every asset and every line of code we ship is made, reviewed and signed off by our own artists and engineers, and the IP in client work belongs to the client.
