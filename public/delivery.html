Delivery policy

Everything we make arrives digitally.

How the work of Anemos Interactive Private Limited is delivered.

Digital only

All our work is delivered digitally: as files, repositories, builds or app store releases, as agreed for the project. No physical goods are shipped.

Timelines

Delivery timelines are set per project in the signed agreement, milestone by milestone.

Confirmation

Each delivery is confirmed by email, so both sides have a record of what was delivered and when.

Games bought on a store

This policy covers the services we provide to clients. Our games are delivered by the platform you buy them on, including but not limited to Steam, the Epic Games Store and the mobile app stores, to your library or device under that platform's own terms.
