About us

A digital-first studio

Anemos Interactive Private Limited is a fully online business. Our team develops, publishes, and delivers all of our work digitally — games distributed through online storefronts, and software services delivered remotely to clients. We are incorporated in Mumbai, Maharashtra, and recognised under the DPIIT Startup India programme.
