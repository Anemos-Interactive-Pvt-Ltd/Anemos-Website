Pricing

How we price.

Every project is quoted on its own terms. Here is how that works.

Custom quotes

All projects are custom-quoted based on scope, timeline and complexity. We do not list fixed prices.

The process

  1. A free consultation call to understand the project.
  2. A written proposal with the scope, timeline and fee.
  3. A signed agreement.
  4. A 10% advance of the total project fee, payable on signing.
  5. The remaining 90% invoiced in milestones as set out in the agreement.

Billing models

Per project, per milestone, or on a retainer, whichever suits the work.

Taxes and invoices

Prices exclude GST, which is added where it applies. Invoices are due within 7 days.

Payment methods

Our payment gateway partners, direct bank transfer (NEFT, RTGS or IMPS), or international wire transfer (SWIFT). The method for each project is stated on its invoice.
